# AdWhispr Competitor Ads

See your competitors' live ads and what works for them, from chat.

Install the plugin, sign in to AdWhispr once (free, no ad account needed), and ask:

- "Who are my competitors running ads right now? My site is example.com."
- "Show me Gymshark's longest-running ads."
- "Does Hims run testimonial ads?"
- "Compare Allbirds and Rothy's."
- "Find keyword ideas for meal prep delivery in Germany, with search volume and cost per click."

## What it does

- **Verified competitors, not guesses.** Brands checked moments ago to be running ads in your space, ranked by how many they have live. For a local business, the ones advertising in your area.
- **The proven winners first.** Ads from the Meta Ad Library (Facebook and Instagram) shown inline with their images and videos, sorted by how long they have been running, because a brand only keeps paying for an ad that works.
- **Search by idea.** Find the before-and-after, founder-story or free-shipping ads inside any brand's library.
- **TikTok too.** A competitor's live TikTok ads, and any account's top posts with real views, likes, comments, shares and saves.
- **Stats, comparisons and a brief.** Ad volume, formats, hooks and longevity for one brand, two or three side by side, or a full competitive brief with a PDF.
- **Keyword intelligence.** Real Google Keyword Planner search volumes and cost-per-click ranges in about 180 countries, plus the keyword themes behind any competitor's site.

## Skills

- **Find and read competitors**: `competitor-research`, `niche-ad-report`, `local-competitor-scan`, `brand-vs-brand`, `new-ad-watch`.
- **The ads themselves**: `longest-running-ads`, `ad-search`, `winning-angle-extractor`, `tiktok-research`, `competitive-brief`.
- **Keywords**: `competitor-keyword-spy`, `keyword-universe-builder`.
- **`get-started`**: the two-minute first run.

## Honest by design

Ad libraries do not publish click rates, conversions or return on ad spend, so this plugin never invents them. Spend and reach are shown as the ranges the libraries report. TikTok's ad library covers ads shown in Europe and has no spend data. Days running is the performance signal.

Research only: it never connects to, launches or changes anything in an ad account. To launch and manage your own campaigns from chat, see the [AdWhispr Ads Manager](https://github.com/adwhispr/ads-manager-plugin) plugin.
